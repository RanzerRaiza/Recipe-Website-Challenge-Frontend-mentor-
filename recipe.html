Simple omelete recipe

An easy and quick dish perfect for any meal.This classic omelete combines beaten eggs cooked to perfection,optionally filled with your choice of cheese,vegetables or meats.

Preparation time

  • Total: approximately 10 minutes
  • Preparation:5 minutes
  • Cooking:5 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2-3 large eggs
  • salt,pepper to taste
  • 1 tablespoon of butter or oil
  • optional fillings: cheese,diced vegetables,meats, herbs

instructions

  1. Beat the eggs: in a bowl beat the eggs with a pinch of salt and pepper until they are well mixed,you can add a spoon of water or milk for a fluffier texture.

  2. Heat the pan:place a non-stick pan on medium heat and add butter or oil.

  3. Cook the eggs:once the butter is melted and bubbling pour the eggs. Tilt the pan and ensure the eggs coat the surface evenly.

  4. Add fillings(optional):when the eggs begin to settle at the edges but are still slightly runny in the middle,sprinkle your chosen fillings on one half of the omelte

  5. Fold and serve: as the Omelete continues to cook,carefully lift oe side and flip it on the other side.

  6. Enjoy:serve hot with salt and pepper if needed.
